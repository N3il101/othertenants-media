Privacy Policy

othertenants — last updated 31 August 2026

What this application is

othertenants is a single-operator publishing tool. It takes short animal videos that its operator has produced and licensed, and posts them to the operator's own social accounts — Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. It is not a consumer product, has no sign-up, and is used by one person: the owner of the @othertenants accounts.

What data it collects

From viewers of the content: nothing. The application has no website, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking and no user accounts. People who watch a video are never seen by this software.

From the operator: an access credential and nothing else. When the operator connects a platform account, that platform issues an OAuth token. The token is stored as an encrypted secret in the project's private build environment and is used only to upload the operator's own videos. No profile information, contact details, follower data, direct messages or audience data are requested, read or retained.

What it does with that data

The token is sent to the issuing platform's own API in order to upload a video. It is not shared with any third party, not sold, not used for advertising, and not processed for any purpose other than publishing the operator's own content to the operator's own account.

How long it is kept

Until it is revoked or replaced. The operator can revoke access at any time from the platform's own settings, which immediately and permanently ends this application's ability to act on the account.

Children

The application is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If what the application does with data changes, this page changes with it and the date above is updated.
